The finding

The day-one pop is real, and it is almost entirely spent in the opening auction. What's left for anyone who buys after the bell rounds, on average, to nothing.

Anatomy · pop, then fade

A lottery you exit early.

Median return against the offer price climbs to about +11% at one month, then decays — −2% by six months, −22% at a year, with barely a third of deals still above water. The mean stays positive only because a handful of listings run enormous; the typical deal does not.

Read plainly: the edge, where one exists, belongs to the subscription seat and the first month — not to buy-and-hold.
